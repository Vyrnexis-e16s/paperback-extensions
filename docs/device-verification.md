# Device verification checklist (Paperback 0.9 (209))

Record pass/fail with date. Do not mark device-verified without doing these on a TestFlight device.

1. Open the 0.9 homepage (`…/0.9/stable/` or `npm run serve`) and tap **Add Repository to Paperback**. Do not use the Inkdex URL.
2. Confirm extensions appear, including at least one Everyone and one Mature 18+ source.
3. Install MangaDex, Webtoon, one manhwa source, and one 18+ source.
4. Search a known public title on each.
5. Open details, confirm cover, description, and chapter list.
6. Open a paginated chapter and a long-strip chapter.
7. Change one reader setting and confirm siblings do not reset.
8. Reload Metadata on a saved title.
9. Open History, Downloads, and New Chapters with a title that has a missing cover.
10. Reopen a title that existed before this registry was added.
11. Confirm a previously downloaded chapter still opens offline.
12. Repeat a subset on 0.9 (208) if that build is still installed. Do not downgrade 209 to 208.
